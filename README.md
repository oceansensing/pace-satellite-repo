# pace-satellite-repo

NASA's PACE satellite's ocean color and atmosphere products from its Ocean
Color Instrument (OCI), as gridded data for the oceansensing.org ocean map:
twenty-two quantities — chlorophyll-a, phytoplankton and particulate carbon,
the phytoplankton communities, net primary production, fluorescence, light
attenuation and available light, absorption and backscatter, aerosols, ozone,
nitrogen dioxide and clouds — each as its newest day and as the mean of each
cell's clear days over the eight newest days, on NASA's 4 km grid (0.1° for
the aerosols).

Updated every two hours.

## Sources and credits

NASA's Ocean Biology Distributed Active Archive Center (OB.DAAC). NASA's data use guidance places mission data under CC0 and asks that it be cited: NASA OB.DAAC, PACE OCI, near-real-time, at the version each header's `stream` names.
